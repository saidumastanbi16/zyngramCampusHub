SENSITIVE BY DEFAULT

  • Salted scrypt password hashes; no source-code passwords.
  • HTTP-only, SameSite session; secure cookies in production.
  • Students see their own records; trainers are scoped to assigned courses.
  • Validation, unique email, and auditable sensitive actions.
  • Keyboard focus, semantic forms, reduced-motion support, mobile-first layout.

Honest boundary: sample-data demo access intentionally skips passwords and is disabled in production. Assessments, official certificates, batch administration, and attendance approvals are not implemented.

z CAMPUS HUB07 · ROADMAP & EVIDENCE

DESIGN REFINEMENTS USE EXPLICIT ASSUMPTIONS; NO EVALUATOR FEEDBACK WAS PROVIDED

Keep the prototype honest.
Make the next release real.

Prototype evidence

Role portals, task submission/review, staff attendance entry, announcements, invited account setup, persistent support tickets and audit views; local tests and production build pass.

Known boundaries

One sample campus; schedule/progress/certificate examples are illustrative. No production deployment, verified SMTP, formal enrollment, file upload, or official assessment.

Roadmap · M0 → M1

Approve privacy, roles and enrollment rules; then configure production identity, managed data, invitation delivery, recovery and session safeguards.

Roadmap · M2 → M3

Replace sample data and formalize sessions/workflows; then test accessibility, security, load, backup recovery and role journeys before a limited pilot.
